Understanding the nature of genes as heritable factors, alleles, and genomes

What is a Gene?

Definition: A gene is a heritable factor that controls a specific characteristic or trait in an organism. Genes are sequences of DNA that code for proteins or functional RNA molecules.

Key Components of Genes

  • DNA Sequence: Genes are made of DNA (deoxyribonucleic acid)
  • Locus: The specific position of a gene on a chromosome
  • Coding Region: The part of the gene that codes for a protein
  • Regulatory Sequences: Control when and where the gene is expressed

Alleles: Different Forms of a Gene

Allele: Alternative versions of the same gene that code for different variations of a trait (e.g., brown eyes vs. blue eyes).

Types of Alleles

Type Description Example
Dominant (A) Expressed when present in either one or two copies Brown eyes (B) over blue eyes (b)
Recessive (a) Only expressed when two copies are present Blue eyes only if genotype is bb
Codominant Both alleles are fully expressed AB blood type (IA and IB both expressed)
Incompletely Dominant Blending of traits in heterozygotes Red + White flowers → Pink flowers

The Genome

Genome: The complete set of genetic material (all DNA) in an organism. Includes both coding and non-coding regions.

Genome Components

  • Nuclear Genome: DNA contained within the nucleus (eukaryotes)
  • Mitochondrial Genome: Small circular DNA in mitochondria (inherited maternally)
  • Chloroplast Genome: DNA in chloroplasts (plants only)
  • Coding DNA: Genes that code for proteins (~1-2% in humans)
  • Non-coding DNA: Regulatory sequences, introns, repetitive DNA

Genome Sizes Comparison

Organism Genome Size (base pairs) Number of Genes
Bacteria (E. coli) ~4.6 million ~4,300
Yeast (S. cerevisiae) ~12 million ~6,000
Fruit fly (D. melanogaster) ~180 million ~14,000
Humans (H. sapiens) ~3.2 billion ~20,000-25,000

Genes vs. Alleles vs. Genome

Term Definition Example
Gene Sequence of DNA coding for a trait Gene for eye color
Allele Variant form of a gene B (brown) or b (blue) for eye color
Genotype Genetic makeup of an organism Bb (heterozygous for eye color)
Phenotype Physical expression of traits Brown eyes
Genome All genetic material in an organism Complete human DNA (~3.2 billion bp)
