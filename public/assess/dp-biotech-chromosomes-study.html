🧬 Chromosomes (Topic 3.2)

IB Diploma BioTechnology - Core SL & HL

Structure of DNA in prokaryotes and eukaryotes, karyotypes, and chromosome numbers

What are Chromosomes?

Definition: Chromosomes are structures made of DNA and proteins (histones) that carry genetic information. They are visible during cell division and contain hundreds to thousands of genes.

Chromosome Structure

DNA Packaging Hierarchy

DNA → Nucleosomes → Chromatin fibers → Chromosomes

  • DNA double helix: 2 nm wide
  • Nucleosome: DNA wrapped around histone proteins (11 nm)
  • Chromatin fiber: Coiled nucleosomes (30 nm)
  • Chromosome: Highly condensed chromatin (700 nm)

Parts of a Chromosome

  • Centromere: Constricted region where sister chromatids join
  • Chromatid: One copy of a duplicated chromosome
  • Telomeres: Protective caps at chromosome ends (prevent degradation)
  • Arms: p-arm (short) and q-arm (long)

Prokaryotes vs. Eukaryotes

Feature Prokaryotes (Bacteria) Eukaryotes (Plants/Animals)
DNA Structure Circular, naked (no histones) Linear, wrapped around histones
Number of Chromosomes Usually 1 (circular DNA) Multiple pairs (diploid: 2n)
Location Nucleoid region (no nucleus) Inside nucleus
Extra DNA Plasmids (small circular DNA) Mitochondrial/Chloroplast DNA
Size Small (~4.6 million bp) Large (~3.2 billion bp in humans)

Karyotypes

Karyotype: A visual display of all chromosomes in a cell, arranged in pairs by size and shape. Used to detect chromosomal abnormalities.

Chromosomal Abnormalities

Condition Chromosome Abnormality Description
Down Syndrome Trisomy 21 Extra copy of chromosome 21 (47 chromosomes total)
Turner Syndrome Monosomy X Only one X chromosome (XO) in females
Klinefelter Syndrome XXY Extra X chromosome in males
Edward Syndrome Trisomy 18 Extra copy of chromosome 18

Haploid vs. Diploid

Diploid (2n): Cells with two sets of chromosomes (one from each parent). Most body cells.
Haploid (n): Cells with one set of chromosomes. Gametes (sperm and egg).

Key Differences

Feature Diploid (2n) Haploid (n)
Chromosome sets 2 sets 1 set
Human chromosome number 46 23
Cell types Somatic (body) cells Gametes (sex cells)
Produced by Mitosis Meiosis
